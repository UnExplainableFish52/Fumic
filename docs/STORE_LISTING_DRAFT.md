## App name

Fumic

## Description

Fumic is a free, open-source media player for your local music and videos on
Windows. Browse your library, search for a track, build playlists, and control
playback with keyboard shortcuts.

Play local audio and video without creating an account. Fumic keeps library
settings, playlists, artwork caches, and visualizer preferences on your computer.
It does not include advertising, telemetry, or cloud sync.

Fumic automatically discovers existing Music and Videos folders under your user
profile on first launch. You can add other library folders in the app. Choose a
file from your library to begin playback, or use Ctrl+Enter to play the selected
item. Use Ctrl+B to add an item to a playlist.

Customize playback with shuffle, repeat, volume, seeking, and speed controls.
Explore the audio visualizer and choose built-in or custom background images.
Fumic also displays metadata and embedded artwork for supported local formats.

This release is designed for Windows desktop PCs with an x64 processor. Media
files are supplied by you; Fumic is a local player and does not provide a media
catalogue or streaming subscription.

## Feature fields

- Local audio and video playback.
- Keyboard shortcuts for playback, search, navigation, playlists, and fullscreen.
- Favourites and custom playlists.
- Local artwork and metadata for supported media formats.
- Shuffle, repeat, seek, volume, and playback speed controls.
- Audio visualizer with built-in and custom backgrounds.
- No account, ads, telemetry, or cloud sync requirement.
- Free and open source under GPLv3.

## What's new

Fumic 2.6.3 improves background playback efficiency, refreshes bundled wallpapers,
and makes player controls and selection highlights clearer. New J/K/L shortcuts,
configurable full-player control hiding, and wallpaper-only playback make it
easier to control music and video from the keyboard.
